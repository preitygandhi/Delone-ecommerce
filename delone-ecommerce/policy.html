Manufacturing faults, internal circuitry failures, charging failure, component defects, and battery hardware malfunctions under normal usage conditions.

  • What is NOT Covered: Physical breakage, water submersion damage, burnt circuits caused by high voltage surges, cut cables, or unauthorized self-repairs.
  • How to Claim Warranty:
    1. Contact our WhatsApp support team at +92 310 0934157.
    2. Share your Order ID / Name along with a short video demonstrating the fault.
